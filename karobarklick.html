KarobarKlick Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026
Developer: Memosha Sinha
Privacy contact: msinha2801@gmail.com

KarobarKlick is an offline demand diary for shopkeepers. It records items customers ask for, groups repeated requests, and helps a shopkeeper decide what to stock next.

Data handled on the device

KarobarKlick does not require an account, password, customer contact list, location, SMS, camera, or network permission. Demand entries, timestamps, and Requested, Ordered, Arrived, or Sold status are stored only in the app’s private device storage.

Voice entry

Voice entry is optional and opens Android’s system speech-recognition prompt. KarobarKlick does not record or store audio. Any handling by the device’s selected speech service is governed by that service’s own settings and privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Users can clear the demand diary inside the app. Uninstalling KarobarKlick removes its private app data from the device.

Sharing

KarobarKlick does not send demand entries to servers or share them with advertisers, analytics providers, suppliers, customers, or other third parties.

Security

KarobarKlick uses Android private app storage for the demand diary. Do not enter payment card details, passwords, or other secrets in demand entries.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app changes. For privacy questions, contact msinha2801@gmail.com.
