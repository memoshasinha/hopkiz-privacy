NoJhol Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026
Developer: Memosha Sinha
Privacy contact: msinha2801@gmail.com

NoJhol is an on-device job-scam safety guide. It provides risk guidance only and does not verify employers, people, phone numbers, payments, documents, or job offers.

Data handled on the device

NoJhol does not require an account, password, phone number, email address, contacts, SMS, call logs, location, camera, or photo access. If a user chooses “Save this check on this device”, the app saves the selected category, risk result, evidence checklist items, and optional notes in private device storage. A user may enter a contact number or UPI ID in an optional note; it stays only on that device. NoJhol does not save the copied job message, link, or screenshot.

Retention and deletion

Users can clear saved check history inside the app. Uninstalling NoJhol removes its private app data. Screenshots or evidence kept outside NoJhol remain on the device and are not controlled by NoJhol.

Sharing

NoJhol does not send user-entered data to servers or share it with advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, employers, recruiters, or other third parties. When a user taps a call, reporting, browser, or share button, Android opens the chosen service; that action is controlled by the user.

Security

NoJhol uses Android private app storage for saved history. Do not enter OTPs, PINs, passwords, card numbers, bank logins, or other secrets into the app.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app changes. For privacy questions, contact msinha2801@gmail.com.
