TuChal Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 September 2026

TuChal is an independent road-journey companion. This version works locally on your device.

Information TuChal collects

TuChal V1 does not collect location, contacts, account credentials, payment information, advertising identifiers, or background activity data. It does not require registration.

Sharing

When you choose Share a journey check-in, TuChal opens an Android sharing screen with a pre-filled message. You choose whether to send it and which app or person receives it. TuChal does not receive or store that message.

Data retention and deletion

TuChal V1 does not operate a user account or cloud storage service. You may remove the app at any time through your device settings.

Third parties

TuChal V1 does not include advertising, analytics, payments, or a map service.
