HopKiz Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 August 2026

HopKiz is a parent-led family activity planner. It offers general activity ideas and is not medical, therapeutic, diagnostic, or emergency advice.

Information stored by the app

HopKiz may store a parent email and password hash for local sign-in, together with parent and child profile details, activity history, reminders, favourites, Family Pulse entries, and parent-managed cart items. This information is stored locally on the device to provide the app's features.

Children and parents

Parents create and control HopKiz profiles. Children do not create accounts, make purchases, contact other users, or share content publicly through the app. Parents should supervise use of the app and choose activities appropriate for their child.

What HopKiz does not collect

This beta does not use advertising, analytics, camera, microphone, contacts, location, or background tracking. HopKiz does not send child profile details or activity data to a HopKiz server.

Sharing and external actions

Family Pulse, weekly summaries, and Parent Meet invitations use the phone's standard share sheet or email app only after a parent chooses to share. HopKiz does not automatically send messages or share data with third parties.

Storage, retention, and deletion

HopKiz keeps its profile information on the device. Android automatic backup is disabled for the app. A parent can use Parent Profile → Privacy & data → Delete account and data to remove the local parent account and associated local child profiles, activity history, reminders, favourites, cart items, and Family Pulse information. Uninstalling the app also removes local app data.

Account deletion request

If you cannot access the app or need deletion support, email msinha2801@gmail.com with the subject HopKiz account deletion request. Because HopKiz does not keep a server-side account database in this beta, we will guide you through removing the local app data from your device.

Security

We use reasonable device-level storage practices for this beta. No method of electronic storage is completely secure; parents should protect access to their device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as HopKiz develops. The effective date above will be revised when changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion support, contact us at msinha2801@gmail.com.
